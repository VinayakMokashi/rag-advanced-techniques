# Kestrel Outfitters Customer Handbook

Kestrel Outfitters is a fictional outdoor-gear shop. Every policy and price in this
handbook is made up, so a language model cannot answer questions about it from memory:
it has to retrieve the right section. `rag14.py` and `rag15.py` use this file as their
knowledge base.

## About Kestrel Outfitters

Kestrel Outfitters sells camping, hiking and paddling gear online and from three stores,
in Boulder, Flagstaff and Bend. Stores are open 9 am to 8 pm Monday to Saturday and
10 am to 6 pm on Sunday. Customer service answers website chat from 8 am to 10 pm every
day.

## Returns and refunds

Kestrel Outfitters accepts returns within 45 days of delivery. A receipt or order number
is required. Tents, sleeping bags and camping stoves that have been used or taken out of
their packaging carry a 12% restocking fee, which is deducted from the refund. GPS
watches, headlamps and other electronics have a shorter 21-day return window and must be
returned with all chargers and accessories. Refunds go back to the original payment
method within 5 business days of the return reaching our warehouse. Canoes and kayaks
can only be returned at a store; we do not collect boats.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 4-6 business days. It is free on orders over $85; smaller orders
pay a flat $7.95. Express shipping delivers in 2 business days for $19, but express is
not available for orders that contain canoes or kayaks. Canoes and kayaks ship by
freight only: freight delivery takes 10-14 business days and costs a flat $149 per boat.
We ship to all 50 US states and do not ship outside the United States.

## Changing or cancelling an order

An order can be changed or cancelled free of charge until it is packed, which usually
happens within 2 hours of the order being placed. Once an order is packed it can no
longer be changed; return the items instead under the normal returns policy.

## Summit Club loyalty programme

Summit Club membership costs $30 a year and includes free express shipping on every
eligible order. Summit Club members earn 3 reward points for every dollar spent;
shoppers without a membership earn 1 point per dollar. Every 500 reward points can be
exchanged for a $20 voucher. Vouchers expire 12 months after they are issued.

## Warranty and repairs

Kestrel-brand jackets come with a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
Products from other brands are covered only by their maker's warranty. Warranty claims
are handled at the in-store Repair Desk. The Repair Desk also replaces broken zippers for
a flat $25 on any jacket, even one that is out of warranty.

## Price matching

Kestrel Outfitters matches a lower price advertised by another retailer within 14 days
of purchase. Prices from auction, marketplace or clearance websites are not matched.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in values from $25 to $500. They never expire and can be used both
online and in stores, but they cannot be exchanged for cash.
